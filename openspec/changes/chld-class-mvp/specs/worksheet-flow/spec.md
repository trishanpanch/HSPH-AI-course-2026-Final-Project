# Worksheet flow requirements

The teacher SHALL supply a complete simple text worksheet, shared goal, grade/age and 1–3 of the eleven needs. Missing essential diagrams SHALL block continuation. Numbered source question references SHALL all appear once in adaptation output. The model SHALL produce a plan before adaptation. AI output SHALL be treated as an unapproved draft and validated on the server.

The teacher SHALL review and edit the worksheet and address concerns. Download SHALL remain disabled until the actual current A4 PDF is previewed and explicitly approved. Any input, plan, draft or layout change SHALL invalidate approval. A late response SHALL NOT overwrite newer work. Failure SHALL preserve inputs and offer recovery. No model key or password SHALL appear in browser code, Git or logs. Missing/wrong password SHALL prevent all AI calls. No fixtures SHALL masquerade as live generation.
