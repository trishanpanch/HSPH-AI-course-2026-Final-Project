A Plant at School

Grade 2 Reading Comprehension • Lexile: 380L (28 words)
Maya brought a small green plant to school on Monday. She placed it by the sunny window in room 4. Each morning, Maya gave the plant water with a yellow cup. Soon, three white flowers bloomed.

1. Who brought the plant to school?

2. Where did Maya put the plant?

3. When did she water the plant?

4. What bloomed on the plant?
