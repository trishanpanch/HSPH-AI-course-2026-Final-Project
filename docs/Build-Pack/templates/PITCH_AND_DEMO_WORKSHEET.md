# Pitch and demo worksheet

Create a short deck about the product the group actually built. Use the original pitch outline only as background.

## Pitch deck

Cover these points, using a slide for each where useful:

1. **Problem:** who experiences it, why it matters and how it is handled today.
2. **User and value:** who the solution serves and the change it aims to make.
3. **Solution:** the focused user journey and the part AI plays, if any.
4. **Demonstration:** show the working product or introduce the live demo.
5. **Evidence and limits:** what the tests showed, what failed and what is simulated.
6. **Next step:** what to test, change or learn before considering a real pilot.

Credit the original idea author and the people who built the class prototype.
