# 03 Build the Solution

## Decision
- **Formal Authorization to Proceed**: We recommend formally approving the CHLD Teacher AI Studio for solution development and pilot deployment targeting the September 2026 community milestone [1]. The opportunity has been shaped and validated around a high-conviction, dual-tool "skateboard" architecture ("Things to Try" and "CHLD Adapt") that directly alleviates mainstream teacher workload in resource-constrained Philippine classrooms without requiring complex prompt engineering [1-2].
- **Strict Non-Diagnostic Scope Boundary**: The product boundary is locked to non-diagnostic, functional classroom accommodations, completely excluding clinical diagnostic classification, developmental labeling, and automated medical advice [2-3]. This boundary directly satisfies CHLD's foundational charter and aligns with the ethical recommendations of the PEARL-AI framework [2-3].
- **Subsidized Zero-Login Initial Pilot Delivery**: For the September 2026 initial launch, CHLD will subsidize AI computational inference costs to provide an open, unauthenticated web utility [1]. This zero-friction approach eliminates onboarding barriers for participating educators while establishing an ephemeral, zero-retention data architecture that prevents pediatric privacy liabilities [2-3].
- **Pre-Pilot Benchmark Quality Gate**: Release of the live web tool is contingent upon passing an offline 50-artifact benchmark evaluated by a multidisciplinary panel (developmental specialist, master teacher, clinical psychologist), requiring an average score >= 4.0/5.0 across 8 core evaluation dimensions and zero critical safety or construct failures [1][3].

## Product Architecture Or Operating Model
- **Front-End Guided Card Interface**: The client application is structured as a lightweight, mobile-responsive web wizard utilizing large selectable cards, clear typography, and concise step-by-step questions rather than an open-ended conversational chat box [1]. To support teachers in schools with intermittent internet connectivity, the client layer incorporates local input caching to prevent text loss during network dropouts and is engineered with minimal heavy assets for rapid loading [2].
- **Client-Side Privacy and Sanitization Pipeline**: Before any input payload is transmitted to the processing layer, an automated client-side sanitization module scrubs student names, birthdates, school identifiers, and contact details [2-3]. The system enforces an unauthenticated session model where no user profiles or persistent student records are created, fulfilling data minimization mandates under PEARL-AI [3].
- **Deterministic Safeguarding Triage Gate**: In the "Things to Try" workflow, all submitted behavioral descriptions pass through a hard-coded deterministic screening layer prior to generative model invocation [1][3]. Inputs matching acute safety triggers—including imminent physical violence, weapon possession, self-harm, suspected abuse or neglect, severe elopement into danger, or medical emergencies—immediately halt strategy generation and display institutional safeguarding referral instructions [1][3].
- **Multi-Agent Orchestration and Knowledge Base Retrieval**: Non-crisis inputs are processed by coordinated backend reasoning agents—incorporating a child development specialist, curriculum assessor, adaptation specialist, and safety reviewer—grounded in a curated CHLD knowledge base covering Universal Design for Learning (UDL), executive function scaffolding, and Philippine classroom context variables [1-2]. The knowledge base is modularly maintainable without requiring AI model fine-tuning [1].
- **Two-Tier Adaptation and Learning Goal Preservation Engine**: In the "CHLD Adapt" workflow, the curriculum assessment agent compares original materials against generated adaptations, evaluating whether modifications represent an "Access change" (e.g., visual chunking, simplified instructions, font enlargement) or a "Demand-changing adaptation" (e.g., converting short-answer recall to multiple-choice recognition) [1]. Demand changes automatically trigger a prominent on-screen warning banner requiring explicit teacher confirmation [1].
- **Classroom-Ready Export and Grayscale Print Engine**: Outputs are formatted into clean, high-contrast HTML and PDF templates optimized for standard black-and-white A4 printing and quick browser export [1-2]. Generated strategy summary cards and adapted worksheets are completely self-contained, allowing teachers to take them offline immediately for direct classroom distribution [1-2].

## Human Roles And Review Points
- **Classroom Educator (Primary In-the-Loop Decision Maker)**: The mainstream teacher remains the authoritative pedagogical agent [2-3]. The teacher inputs observable classroom behaviors or uploads existing worksheets, reviews generated strategies against their specific classroom dynamics, explicitly acknowledges any cognitive-demand shift alerts, and makes final editorial adjustments before printing [1].
- **Expert Clinical and Educational Review Panel**: Prior to pilot deployment, a 4-person multidisciplinary panel (developmental pediatrician/allied health professional, 2 master teachers/curriculum experts, and 1 child rights/safeguarding specialist) executes the standardized 50-artifact benchmark audit to validate clinical boundaries, construct fidelity, and safety triage precision [2-3].
- **Institutional School Leadership and Safeguarding Officers**: When the safeguarding triage gate intercepts an acute crisis input, the system directs the educator to immediately engage designated school administrators, guidance counselors, or child protection committees according to established institutional procedures [1-2].
- **User Redress and Continuous Audit Telemetry**: Every generated output card includes a persistent "Flag Output" and "Report Concern" mechanism, providing teachers an accessible channel to report inappropriate advice, unintended translation, or formatting defects [3]. Ephemeral, de-identified error logs are reviewed weekly by the CHLD product and child-development team to refine knowledge base rules [2-3].

## Validation Status
- **Validation Plan Completion**: The validation methodology designed in Stage 02 established rigorous success criteria: >= 80% teacher usability rating, >= 90% construct preservation accuracy, 100% precision on safety triage hard-stops, and mean generation time under 3 minutes [1][3].
- **Benchmark Framework Operationalization**: An 8-dimension expert scoring rubric (Construct Preservation, Appropriateness of Adaptation, Safety/Escalation, Non-Diagnostic Framing, Practical Usefulness, Clarity/Accessibility, Factual Accuracy, Teacher Control) has been formally codified with binary critical failure gates [1][3].
- **Bilingual and Contextual Compatibility**: The pipeline architecture has been validated against Philippine classroom language requirements, demonstrating full comprehension of English, Filipino/Tagalog, and colloquial Taglish inputs while strictly preserving the source language of uploaded academic materials [1-2].
- **Privacy and Data Minimization Verification**: The end-to-end technical model has been structured for zero application-level data retention, utilizing client-side sanitization, ephemeral in-memory execution, disabled session logging, and zero-retention API provider contracts in compliance with PEARL-AI guidelines [2-3].

## Evidence Versus Interpretation
- **Supplied Facts**: CHLD operates within the Philippine education ecosystem characterized by large class sizes, heavy teacher workloads, and scarce developmental specialists [2][1]. Standard adult AI ethics frameworks are inadequate for pediatric use, necessitating human-in-the-loop oversight, fail-safe boundaries, and data minimization as defined in PEARL-AI literature [3]. General-purpose LLMs routinely alter cognitive task demands when simplifying educational texts unless constrained [1].
- **Strategic Interpretation**: We interpret that a guided, card-based interface requiring zero prompt engineering will achieve substantially higher adoption and implementation fidelity among Philippine teachers than open chatbot prompts [1-2]. We further interpret that institutional buyers (private school networks and LGUs) will prioritize an ethically defensible, training-integrated inclusion utility over generic commercial AI generators [1-2].

## Unresolved Uncertainty
- **B2B/B2G Procurement and Budgetary Cycles**: While initial pilot usage is subsidized by CHLD, the exact commercial conversion timelines and procurement mechanisms across Philippine private school chains and local government education funds remain subject to institutional budget cycles and pilot efficacy evidence [1-2].
- **OCR Parsing Edge Cases for Low-Quality Scans**: The parsing fidelity of heavily degraded, low-contrast photocopied worksheets or non-standard tabular test layouts may require fallback to direct text pasting during the initial pilot phase [1].
- **Longitudinal Behavioral Impact in Classrooms**: The long-term efficacy of AI-suggested classroom accommodations in reducing chronic behavioral distress across multi-week periods requires ongoing observational evaluation during post-pilot school partnerships [2-3].
- **Teacher Adherence to Safeguarding Redirection**: While the software deterministically halts generation during crisis inputs, institutional adherence depends on clear school-level communication and training regarding mandatory child protection reporting [1-2].

## Accepted Risks
- **Subsidized Computational Cost Overhead**: CHLD accepts the variable computational inference cost of providing free, unauthenticated access during the September 2026 pilot in order to maximize user adoption, collect real-world usability data, and validate product-market fit before instituting paid tiers [1].
- **Potential Teacher Demand-Change Confirmation Fatigue**: Requiring teachers to manually confirm and accept cognitive demand alterations in "CHLD Adapt" introduces minor interface friction, but this friction is deliberately accepted to safeguard academic rigor and prevent accidental construct dilution [1].
- **Scope Restriction to Dual-Tool Skateboard**: By intentionally omitting user accounts, student history tracking, and complex lesson planning modules, CHLD accepts a narrower initial feature set to guarantee exceptional reliability, safety, and rapid deployment [1-2].

## Continue Narrow Pivot Pause Stop Or Override
- **Recommendation: Continue**: Proceed directly to solution development, benchmark test execution, and deployment preparation for the September 2026 pilot [1].
- **Scope Guardrails**: Maintain the narrow dual-tool focus ("Things to Try" and "CHLD Adapt"); do not expand into multi-tool lesson planning platforms, user account management, or longitudinal student tracking before post-pilot evaluation [1-2].
- **Operational Trigger**: If pre-pilot benchmark testing reveals an average score < 4.0/5.0 or any critical safety failure, narrow "CHLD Adapt" to plain-text visual reformatting and halt generative behavioral strategy output until prompt schemas are revalidated [1][3].

- **Benchmark Execution and Pitch Deck Finalization**: Conduct the blinded 50-case benchmark evaluation with the multidisciplinary expert panel to verify zero critical failures, finalize the companion pitch-deck artifact, and prepare the unauthenticated web release for the September 2026 community milestone [1][3].
