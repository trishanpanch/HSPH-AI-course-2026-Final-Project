# 02 Test the Opportunity

## Highest-Risk Assumptions
- **Educational Construct Preservation vs. Accessibility**: Generative AI adaptations in "CHLD Adapt" risk unintentionally diluting academic rigor when attempting to improve visual or reading accessibility [1]. Language models frequently alter open-ended recall tasks into recognition tasks (such as converting short-answer questions into multiple-choice items) or strip core disciplinary vocabulary when simplifying language. This matters because if adapted materials change what the learner is required to demonstrate rather than merely how they access the task, teachers cannot validly assess student mastery. The product validation must verify whether explicit rule-based intent checks and visible "Changes task demand" warnings enable teachers to maintain curricular integrity [1].
- **Safety Triage and Non-Diagnostic Boundary Enforcement**: Mainstream Philippine teachers operating under high student-to-teacher ratios and acute time pressure may submit descriptions involving severe distress, physical violence, suspected abuse, or self-harm into "Things to Try" [2], [1]. If the system responds to acute crises with ordinary classroom accommodation strategies instead of halting immediately, it violates non-maleficence and child safety standards established under the PEARL-AI framework [3]. We must test whether deterministic escalation rules reliably intercept acute safeguarding and medical emergencies while keeping lower-level behavioral suggestions strictly functional and non-diagnostic [2], [3].
- **Bilingual Taglish Intake Comprehension and Contextual Fidelity**: Philippine educators routinely switch between English and Filipino (Taglish) when articulating classroom challenges or submitting instructional worksheets [2], [1]. If the underlying language pipeline fails to parse colloquial bilingual descriptions accurately or attempts to automatically translate academic materials into unnatural phrasing, teacher trust will collapse. We must validate whether the system can comprehend mixed-language inputs and generate actionable strategies in the teacher's primary language while preserving the original source language of instructional materials [1].
- **Zero-Login Privacy Hygiene and PII Leakage Prevention**: In an unauthenticated, zero-friction web utility, educators frequently paste real student names, medical labels, or institutional identifiers into open text fields despite explicit warning banners [1], [3]. Under PEARL-AI guidelines, pediatric systems must enforce strict data minimization and eliminate persistent storage of re-identifiable child data [3]. The testing plan must confirm that client-side scrubbing, ephemeral in-memory processing, zero database persistence, and verified zero-retention AI API configurations eliminate pediatric privacy risks without adding user friction [2], [3].
- **Low-Bandwidth Execution and Offline Utility Delivery**: Philippine school environments frequently experience unstable connectivity and rely on basic monochrome printers [2]. If the web studio requires heavy assets, continuous connectivity during editing, or produces colorful, image-heavy layouts that render illegibly on black-and-white A4 prints, adoption will fail. We must validate that lightweight web pages, local form draft caching, and clean grayscale printable exports function seamlessly under bandwidth constraints [2], [1].
- **Workflow Speed and Adoption Over Generic Chatbot Baselines**: Mainstream classroom teachers with limited preparation time and varying digital literacy will abandon standalone utilities if the multi-step guided intake is slower or more cumbersome than ad-hoc workarounds or generic chatbots [2], [1]. We must test whether a card-based questionnaire requiring zero prompt engineering delivers a classroom-ready strategy card or printable differentiated worksheet in under 3 minutes [1].

## Validation And Invalidation Conditions
- **Validation Success Conditions**: The opportunity is validated for pilot progression if the pre-pilot benchmark achieves an average expert panel score of >= 4.0/5.0 with zero critical failures; at least 80% of participating pilot teachers rate outputs as classroom-ready in under 3 minutes without prompting assistance; 100% of acute safeguarding/safety test cases trigger immediate hard stops to institutional emergency procedures; expert review confirms at least 90% construct preservation fidelity in adapted worksheets; and zero clinical diagnostic labels are emitted [1], [3].
- **Invalidation Conditions**: The concept is invalidated in its current architecture if any single critical failure occurs (such as unsafe behavioral advice, missed safeguarding escalation, diagnostic labeling, unflagged curricular construct degradation, or answer leakage); if more than 10% of adapted materials alter cognitive assessment demands without triggering prominent warning flags; or if teachers spend more time manually reformatting generated materials than preparing them by hand [1], [3].

## Smallest Useful Learning Test
- **Blinded Pre-Pilot Benchmark (50 Synthetic & Historical Cases)**: Construct an offline benchmark dataset comprising 25 behavioral scenarios for "Things to Try" (spanning attention hurdles, transition distress, motor fatigue, and acute safety edge cases) and 25 instructional artifacts for "CHLD Adapt" (encompassing reading passages, math tasks, and bilingual science worksheets) [2], [1]. A multidisciplinary panel (developmental pediatrician, master teachers, child psychologist) evaluates pipeline outputs on a 1–5 scale across 8 core dimensions: Construct Preservation, Appropriateness of Adaptation, Safety and Escalation, Non-Diagnostic Framing, Practical Usefulness, Clarity and Accessibility, Factual/Instructional Accuracy, and Teacher Control/Transparency [2], [3]. Binary "critical failure" gates are enforced, requiring >= 4.0/5.0 average score and 0 critical failures before release [1].
- **September 2026 Interactive Community Pilot**: Deploy an unauthenticated, mobile-responsive web studio at the CHLD September 2026 community-building event, providing subsidized inference for 30–50 practicing Philippine educators [1]. The interface is optimized for low-bandwidth environments (text-first, minimal animations, standard system fonts, compressed uploads), with client-side draft caching to protect entered text against intermittent connectivity drops [2], [1].
- **Classroom Implementation Follow-up (10-Day Cohort Trial)**: Engage 15 pilot teachers across partner private and public schools to implement generated strategy checklists and adapted worksheets during active class instruction [2], [1]. Teachers evaluate offline usability, grayscale print legibility on standard A4 paper, and log 1-minute daily feedback on whether accommodations were practical, whether student engagement improved, and whether outputs required manual correction [1].
- **Safety and Usability Redress Channel**: Embed an on-screen reporting tool ("Flag Output" / "Report Concern") on every result card, enabling educators to immediately report inappropriate advice, unexpected language translation, or formatting errors in accordance with PEARL-AI auditability standards [3].

## Participants Or Audience
- **Target Educator Participants**: 30–50 early childhood and primary educators (Kindergarten to Grade 4) in the Philippines, encompassing mainstream general education teachers managing inclusive classes and school learning-support staff [2], [1].
- **Multidisciplinary Review Panel**: A 4-person expert evaluation group comprising 1 developmental pediatrician or child health specialist, 2 senior curriculum and instructional design experts, and 1 child rights/safeguarding advisor [2], [3].

## Test Workflow
- **Intake and Orientation**: Teachers access the studio URL without login, view an explicit notice stating that the utility is non-diagnostic and does not store student information, and select either "Things to Try" or "CHLD Adapt" [1], [3].
- **Guided Input & Safety Screening**: For "Things to Try," users select observable behaviors and answer 2–3 contextual questions (setting, triggers, prior supports); inputs are scanned against the deterministic safeguarding rule set [1]. For "CHLD Adapt," users paste text or upload an artifact and designate the accessibility barrier [1]. Input text is preserved locally in the browser to prevent data loss during network interruptions [2].
- **Review & Demand Verification**: In "CHLD Adapt," the system classifies the output into "Access change: The learning task has been retained" or "This changes the task demand," requiring teacher confirmation before downloading [1].
- **Export and Micro-Survey**: The educator exports the formatted artifact (compact printable strategy card or black-and-white A4 worksheet via browser print or PDF download) and answers a 3-question survey measuring time saved, usability, and likelihood of classroom deployment [1].

## Evidence To Collect
- **Benchmark Dimension Scores & Critical Failure Rate**: Quantitative scores (1–5) across all 8 evaluation dimensions, documenting 0 critical failure occurrences in the 50-case benchmark dataset [1], [3].
- **Safeguarding Triage Accuracy**: Percentage of simulated and live high-risk inputs (serious harm, weapons, self-harm, suspected abuse, medical emergencies) correctly halted and redirected to institutional escalation protocols (target: 100%) [3].
- **Cognitive Demand Classification Fidelity**: Precision and recall of automated tagging distinguishing between access modifications and task-demand alterations against expert panel ground truth (target: >= 90%) [1].
- **Bilingual Processing Reliability**: Efficacy rate of Taglish and Filipino input parsing and adherence to generating output in the user's primary language without unauthorized academic translation [1].
- **End-to-End Task Duration**: Mean time from site entry to printable export, compared to manual preparation and unguided LLM prompting [1].
- **Low-Bandwidth and Offline Usability**: Success rate of grayscale A4 printing, browser print fallback, and artifact utility when used offline in classroom settings [2], [1].
- **Zero-Retention Audit Verification**: Verification logs confirming zero persistence of user inputs, uploaded files, or generated outputs across servers, databases, and AI API endpoints [2], [3].

## Decision Thresholds
- **Proceed to Full Studio Development (Green Light)**: Average benchmark score >= 4.0/5.0 with 0 critical failures; >= 80% educator satisfaction rating outputs as immediately usable; >= 90% accuracy in cognitive demand tagging; 100% precision on safety triage hard stops; mean time-to-output < 3 minutes; and verified zero server-side PII retention [1], [3].
- **Iterate and Refine Prompt/Rule Architecture (Yellow Light)**: Average benchmark score between 3.5 and 3.9 (with 0 critical failures), usability satisfaction between 65% and 79%, or cognitive demand classification accuracy between 75% and 89%, triggering adjustments to knowledge base guidance and prompt guardrails [1].
- **Halt or Narrow Tool Scope (Red Light)**: Any occurrence of a critical failure (unsafe behavioral advice, missed safeguarding escalation, clinical diagnostic labeling, unflagged construct alteration, answer leakage); average benchmark score < 3.5; cognitive demand accuracy < 75%; or severe document parsing failure in mixed-language formats [1], [3]. If triggered, CHLD will halt generative behavioral advice or restrict "CHLD Adapt" strictly to text pasting and visual reformatting [2], [1].

## Provisional Requirements
- **PR-01: Guided Card-Based Interface**: Multi-step, non-technical wizard presenting structured choices and short questions on desktop and mobile browsers [1].
- **PR-02: Curated Safeguarding Hard-Stop Filter**: Deterministic keyword and intent filter that halts generation on acute harm, weapons, self-harm, abuse, or medical emergencies, displaying school safeguarding referral procedures [1], [3].
- **PR-03: Two-Tier Adaptation Classification**: Automated categorization labeling outputs as either "Access change: The learning task has been retained" or "This changes the task demand," with prominent UI confirmation prompts on demand shifts [1].
- **PR-04: Non-Diagnostic Framing Engine**: System prompts grounded in child-development principles that exclude diagnostic labels (ADHD, ASD, dyslexia) and emphasize observable classroom accommodations [2], [1].
- **PR-05: Bilingual and Code-Switching Ingestion**: Natural language pipeline supporting English, Tagalog, and Taglish inputs, outputting advice in the teacher's language while retaining source material language [1].
- **PR-06: Zero-Retention and PII Redaction Layer**: Client-side name/identifier scrubber, ephemeral server processing, zero database persistence, disabled session logging, and strict AI provider data-discard configuration [2], [3].
- **PR-07: Low-Bandwidth and Offline-Print Formatter**: Lightweight text-first layouts, local input draft caching, simple icons over heavy imagery, standard system fonts, browser print support, and high-contrast grayscale A4 print stylesheets for worksheets and strategy cards [2], [1].

## What Not To Build Yet
- **User Authentication and Profiles**: No educator account creation, password management, or institutional admin portals for the September 2026 pilot [1].
- **Longitudinal Student Records**: No persistent student tracking, behavioral case history logs, or individualized education plan (IEP) storage, strictly enforcing data minimization [2], [3].
- **Offline LLM Inference**: No local offline model execution; the MVP uses a brief online connection for generation, after which artifacts remain completely usable offline [2], [1].
- **Proprietary LLM Fine-Tuning**: No model training or parameter fine-tuning; domain specialization is achieved entirely via curated knowledge retrieval and multi-agent prompt orchestration [2], [1].

## Stop Or Narrow Signals
- **Stop Signal**: Inability to reliably halt generation during acute child safeguarding crises, any unflagged construct alteration altering assessment validity, or persistent generation of clinical diagnostic labels during testing [2], [3].
- **Narrow Signal**: Persistent OCR or formatting degradation on complex scanned worksheet layouts, which will narrow "CHLD Adapt" to raw text input and visual restyling [1].
