## Summary
The opportunity frame faithfully captures CHLD's vision for a lightweight, guided Teacher AI Studio comprising two core tools: "Things to Try" (functional, non-diagnostic classroom behavioral and participation strategies) and "CHLD Adapt" (material differentiation that safeguards underlying learning objectives). It firmly anchors the concept within the resource-constrained Philippine educational landscape, aligns with child-centered medical AI ethics (PEARL-AI framework), and avoids premature feature bloat.

## Confirmed Facts
- CHLD operates in the Philippines, incubated at AIM-Dado Banatao Incubator, focusing on early childhood and primary inclusion [1], [2].
- Pediatric AI systems require strict non-maleficence, non-diagnostic framing, human-in-the-loop agency, and data minimization [3].
- Initial distribution targets a zero-friction September 2026 pilot without mandatory user accounts [2].

## Assumptions
- Philippine classroom teachers will adopt a standalone web utility over generic chatbots if prompt friction is eliminated [2].
- Multi-agent or structured prompt orchestration can reliably distinguish between modifying access to a task and changing cognitive learning demands [2].

## Open Questions
- What specific triage thresholds escalate severe behavioral or safety concerns away from classroom tips to institutional safeguarding [2], [3]?
- Which initial adaptation primitives (e.g., visual chunking vs. lexical simplification) yield the highest immediate classroom utility [2]?

## Risks
- Accidental cognitive target degradation in adapted worksheets [2].
- Over-reliance on automated behavioral guidance during acute safety crises [2], [3].

## Recommendations
- Formally isolate non-diagnostic behavioral triage and test prompt/rule schemas for learning objective preservation during the Test stage.
