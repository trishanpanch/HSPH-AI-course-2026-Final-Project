# Development Pack

## What This Pack Is
This Development Pack is the comprehensive product design, validation, and decision-packaging record for the **CHLD Teacher AI Studio** [1-2]. Developed by Companion Hub for Learning and Discovery (CHLD) at the Asian Institute of Management's Dado Banatao Incubator (AIM-DBI), the Studio is a lightweight, guided web utility designed to help classroom educators in the Philippines support learners with diverse developmental and learning needs [2][1]. It delivers two focused, non-diagnostic tools: **Things to Try** (actionable classroom behavioral strategies with observable checkpoints) and **CHLD Adapt** (worksheet and instructional task differentiation that strictly preserves curricular constructs) [1].

## How To Review It
Reviewers should inspect the artifacts in sequential order to evaluate opportunity framing, empirical test criteria, operating architecture, and locked product decisions:
1. `01_frame_opportunity.md`: Strategic opportunity analysis, problem definition, and initial critique [1-2].
2. `02_validate_learning.md`: Highest-risk assumptions, 8-dimension evaluation rubric, pre-pilot benchmark design, and validation conditions [1][3].
3. `03_package_decision.md`: Operating model, multi-agent architecture, safeguarding triage, privacy engineering, and final go-decision [1-3].
4. `decision_log.md`: Locked product and architecture decisions across all workflow stages [1-2].
5. `open_questions.json`: Complete record of evaluated and resolved product uncertainties [1][3].
